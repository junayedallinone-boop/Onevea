[ 01 ] — MISSION

Eyes didn't evolve for neon streets, screens or snow glare. Onevea adds one quiet layer that adapts for them, so you see the world as it really is in any light.

[ 02 ] — HOW WE GOT HERE
2023 · Q1

A lab in the dark

We started by testing low-light lens film in a basement lab, at night, on our own eyes.

2024 · Q3

The adaptive cell

Our electrochromic lens cell reached a 0.2 second response. Glare became something the lens handles, not you.

2025 · Q2

Optic Shell

We brought the frame to 21 grams by combining titanium and plant-based polymer. It took 140 prototypes.

2026 · Q4

Onevea one

Our first pair goes into the world: four layers, one view, 18 hours on a charge.

[ 03 ] — PRINCIPLES
V/01

Invisible tech

The best interface is the one you forget. Onevea has no screens and sends no notifications. You just see better.

V/02

Built to last

Every part can be repaired and every lens can be replaced. The frame is designed to outlive the electronics.

V/03

Your data, your eyes

Iris keys and focus maps stay on the device. Nothing about your eyes leaves the frame.
